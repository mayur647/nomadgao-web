Goa works for remote work if you plan around three things: the season, the power and the scooter.

## When to come

- **October to February:** dry, warm and busy. Most events, markets and music happen now, and prices climb around Christmas and New Year.
- **March to May:** hotter and quieter. Good for long focused stays.
- **June to September (monsoon):** green, rainy, cheaper and calm. Expect occasional power cuts, so choose a place with backup power. Read [why the monsoon is a great time for a workation](/blog/goa-monsoon-workation/).

## Where to stay

North Goa (Anjuna, Assagao, Siolim, Vagator) has the largest remote-work community. Compare areas in [the best places to stay in Anjuna for remote workers](/blog/where-to-stay-anjuna-remote-workers/).

## Internet and power

Ask for measured speeds, a second line and power backup before you book. A local SIM is your safety net.
