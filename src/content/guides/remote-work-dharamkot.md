Dharamkot is a small village above McLeodganj in the Dhauladhar range. It is quiet, walkable and full of cafés, with trails starting from the edge of the village.

## Seasons

- **March to June:** clear days and cool nights. Peak season for hikers.
- **July to September:** monsoon. Heavy rain, mist and fewer visitors.
- **October and November:** crisp, clear and many people's favourite.
- **December to February:** cold, sometimes snowy. Bring proper layers.

## Getting there

Gaggal (Kangra) airport is the closest airfield. Overnight buses run from Delhi to McLeodganj. From McLeodganj it is a short taxi ride or an uphill walk.

## Working here

Mountain weather can affect connectivity, so pick a stay with a backup line and power backup. [NomadGao Dharamkot](/stays/dharamkot/) has a rooftop coworking space facing the mountains.

Hike with a local guide (we run one most Sundays: see [Happenings](/happenings/)), walk down to McLeodganj for the monastery and markets, or take a longer trek with an overnight stay.
